Title: Temple Etiquette and Dress Code at Angkor

Topic: Angkor Temple Dress Code and Etiquette
Language Support: English & Khmer (ភាសាខ្មែរ)

Description:
When visiting the sacred temples of the Angkor Archaeological Park, visitors must strictly respect local religious customs. 
1. Dress Code: Both men and women must cover their shoulders and knees. Sleeveless shirts, tank tops, revealing necklines, and short shorts/skirts are strictly forbidden. You cannot simply cover shoulders with a scarf; actual shirts with sleeves are required.
2. Sacred Behavior: Do not touch, sit on, or lean against ancient carvings or fragile sandstone structures. 
3. Respecting Monks: Monks are highly revered in Cambodia. If you wish to take a photo with a monk, always ask for permission first. Women are strictly forbidden from touching monks or their robes.
4. Noise Levels: Keep voices down. Temples are active places of worship for local Buddhists.
5. Smoking & Littering: Smoking is strictly prohibited throughout the entire archaeological park. Please dispose of all trash in designated bins to keep the UNESCO world heritage site clean.
