Title: Essential Khmer Phrases for Travelers

Topic: Useful Khmer Vocabulary and Greetings
Language Support: English & Khmer (ភាសាខ្មែរ)

Description:
Speaking a few words of Khmer goes a long way in building rapport with locals in Siem Reap.
1. Hello: "Choum Reap Sour" (ជំរាបសួរ) - Formal / respectful greeting, accompanied by the 'Sampeah' (placing hands together in a prayer-like gesture at chest level).
2. Hello (Casual): "Sues Dei" (សួស្តី) - Used among friends or peers.
3. Thank You: "Arkun" (អរគុណ) - To express deep gratitude, say "Arkun Chran" (អរគុណច្រើន - Thank you very much).
4. How much is this?: "Tlai Bonman?" (ថ្លៃប៉ុន្មាន?) - Essential for shopping in local markets.
5. Too expensive!: "Tlai Nas!" (ថ្លៃណាស់!) - Used playfully during friendly bargaining.
6. Can you discount?: "Chos Tlai Ban Te?" (ចុះថ្លៃបានទេ?)
7. Goodbye: "Choum Reap Lea" (ជម្រាបលា) - Formal goodbye.
8. No spicy: "Ot Hal" (អត់ហិល) - Important when ordering food if you cannot tolerate spicy dishes.
9. Delicious: "Chhnganh" (ឆ្ងាញ់) - Always appreciated by local chefs and food stall owners.
10. Excuse me / Sorry: "Som Toh" (សុំទោស)

Khmer Translation / Summary:
ភាសាខ្មែរជាមូលដ្ឋានសម្រាប់ភ្ញៀវទេសចរ៖

Title: Emergency Contacts and Hospitals in Siem Reap

Topic: Emergency Numbers and Quality Healthcare Services
Language Support: English & Khmer (ភាសាខ្មែរ)

Description:
In case of unforeseen emergencies during your trip to Siem Reap, keep these contacts and medical facilities on hand:
1. Primary Emergency Numbers:
   - Police: 117
   - Tourist Police (Siem Reap): (+855) 12 402 424 / (+855) 12 969 991
   - Fire Department: 118
   - Ambulance / Medical Emergency: 119
2. Recommended Hospitals (International Standard):
   - Royal Angkor International Hospital: Part of the Bangkok Hospital Group. Offers high-quality, 24/7 emergency medical services, English-speaking staff, and direct billing with major international travel insurances. Location: National Road 6. Contact: (+855) 63 761 888.
   - Angkor Hospital for Children (AHC): A highly respected non-profit pediatric hospital. While it focuses on local children, it is a key landmark in town. Location: Oum Chhay Street.
3. Pharmacies: Major reputable pharmacies like 'U-Care Pharmacy' are located around the Old Market and Pub Street areas, stocking reliable, non-counterfeit western medications.
