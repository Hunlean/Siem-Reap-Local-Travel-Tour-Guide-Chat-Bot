Title: Currency, Cash, and Payments in Cambodia

Topic: Money Guide (USD, Khmer Riel, and Digital Payments)
Language Support: English & Khmer (ភាសាខ្មែរ)

Description:
Cambodia operates on a dual-currency system, though local digital payment systems have recently dominated.
1. Dual Currencies: Both the US Dollar (USD) and the Cambodian Riel (KHR) are accepted everywhere. The exchange rate is generally pegged at 1 USD = 4,000 KHR. 
2. Change in Riel: If you pay in USD, any change under $1 USD will be returned to you in Khmer Riel. For example, if you are owed $0.50, you will receive 2,000 KHR.
3. Strict USD Quality Rules: Physical US dollar bills must be in pristine condition. Even a tiny tear, fold, pen mark, or heavy wear can result in the bill being rejected by local shops, restaurants, and hotels. Ensure your USD bills are crisp and clean.
4. Bakong & KHQR: Cambodia has a highly advanced national digital payment network called Bakong. Most merchants—from high-end hotels to street food stalls—display a 'KHQR' code. Tourists can set up local e-wallets or use linked international apps to scan and pay instantly.
5. ATMs: ATMs are widely available in Siem Reap city center, dispensing both USD and KHR. Be aware of local ATM withdrawal fees, which usually range from $4 to $6 per transaction.
