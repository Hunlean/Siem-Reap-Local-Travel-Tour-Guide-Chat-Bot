Name (EN): Phare, The Cambodian Circus
Name (KM): ហ្វារ (សៀកកម្ពុជា)
Category: Culture / Entertainment
Latitude: 13.3516

Description:
More than just a circus, Phare utilizes modern theater, music, dance, and breathtaking acrobatics to tell uniquely Cambodian stories—historical, folk, and modern. It is an energetic, highly acclaimed social enterprise that funds education for disadvantaged youth.
