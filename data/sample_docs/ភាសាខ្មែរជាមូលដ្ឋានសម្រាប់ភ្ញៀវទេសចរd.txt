Title: SIM Cards, Mobile Networks, and Internet Access

Topic: Getting Connected: Local SIM Cards and Mobile Operators
Language Support: English & Khmer (ភាសាខ្មែរ)

Description:
Staying connected in Siem Reap is cheap and straightforward. 
1. Key Mobile Network Operators:
   - Smart Axiata: Excellent coverage in Siem Reap city center, fast 4G/LTE speeds, and highly affordable tourist packages.
   - Cellcard: One of the oldest operators with solid coverage, especially in rural areas.
   - Metfone: Widely praised for having the strongest coverage in remote temple areas deep within the archaeological park.
2. Getting a Tourist SIM: You can purchase a tourist SIM card directly at the Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport (SAI) arrival terminal or at official brand stores in town. A passport is strictly required by law to register any local SIM card.
3. Pricing: A standard tourist package costs between $5 and $10 USD, yielding 10GB to 40GB of high-speed 4G data valid for 7 to 30 days.
4. Public Wi-Fi: Almost all cafes, restaurants, hotels, and guesthouses in Siem Reap provide free, reasonably fast Wi-Fi for guests.
