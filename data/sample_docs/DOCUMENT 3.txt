Name (EN): Ta Prohm
Name (KM): ប្រាសាទតាព្រហ្ម
Category: Historical Site
Latitude: 13.4347
Longitude: 103.8892

Description:
Famous for the massive silk-cotton and strangler fig trees entwined with its ruins. Left largely in the state it was discovered, this temple provides a highly atmospheric, romantic experience of nature reclaiming human architecture. Often recognized from its appearance in the Tomb Raider film.
