Name (EN): Kampong Phluk
Name (KM): កំពង់ភ្លុក
Category: Local Village
Latitude: 13.2081
Longitude: 103.9749

Description:
A cluster of three villages of stilted houses built within the floodplain of the Tonle Sap. The houses rise up to 10 meters in the air. During the wet season, the village appears to float, while the dry season reveals the towering stilts. It features a unique flooded mangrove forest.
