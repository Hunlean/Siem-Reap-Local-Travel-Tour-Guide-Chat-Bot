Name (EN): Phnom Bakheng
Name (KM): ប្រាសាទភ្នំបាខែង
Category: Historical Site
Latitude: 13.4239
Longitude: 103.856

Description:
A Hindu and Buddhist temple in the form of a temple mountain. Built at the end of the 9th century, it is situated on a hill and is one of the most popular spots in Siem Reap for viewing the sunset over Angkor Wat and the surrounding forested plains.
