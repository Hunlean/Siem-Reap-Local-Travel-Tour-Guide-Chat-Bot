Name (EN): Road 60 Street Food Market
Name (KM): ផ្សារម្ហូបអាហារផ្លូវ៦០
Category: Street Food
Latitude: 13.3768
Longitude: 103.8732

Description:
A lively local evening market that stretches along Road 60. It is a fantastic place to sample authentic Cambodian street food, including grilled chicken, stuffed frogs, traditional sweets, and an array of edible insects. It operates in the late afternoon and evening.
