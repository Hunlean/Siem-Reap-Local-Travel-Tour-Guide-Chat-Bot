Name (EN): Phnom Kulen National Park
Name (KM): ឧទ្យានជាតិភ្នំគូលែន
Category: Nature / Historical
Latitude: 13.6231
Longitude: 104.1084

Description:
Considered the most sacred mountain in Cambodia and the birthplace of the ancient Khmer Empire. Visitors can explore the River of a Thousand Lingas with its ancient riverbed carvings, a large reclining Buddha at Preah Ang Thom, and picturesque tiered waterfalls perfect for swimming.
