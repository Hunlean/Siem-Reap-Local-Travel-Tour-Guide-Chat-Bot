Name (EN): Bayon Temple
Name (KM): ប្រាសាទបាយ័ន
Category: Historical Site
Latitude: 13.4413
Longitude: 103.8587

Description:
Located in the center of Angkor Thom, this state temple of Jayavarman VII is instantly recognizable by its multitude of serene, smiling stone faces towering over the complex. The bas-reliefs on the outer gallery depict everyday life and historical battles of the Khmer Empire.
