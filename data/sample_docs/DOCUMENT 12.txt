Name (EN): Wat Damnak
Name (KM): វត្តដምናក់
Category: Culture / Religious
Latitude: 13.3541
Longitude: 103.8588

Description:
The largest pagoda in Siem Reap and formerly a royal palace. Today, it is a thriving center of Buddhist learning and houses the Center for Khmer Studies, which operates an extensive library open to the public. The surrounding streets are famous for local street food vendors.
