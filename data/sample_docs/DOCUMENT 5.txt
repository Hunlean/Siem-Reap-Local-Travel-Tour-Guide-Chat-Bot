Name (EN): Preah Khan
Name (KM): ប្រាសាទព្រះខ័ន
Category: Historical Site
Latitude: 13.4619
Longitude: 103.8718

Description:
A vast, flat temple complex built by Jayavarman VII to honor his father. It served as a city and a place of Buddhist and Hindu worship. The site features long, atmospheric corridors, excellent carvings, and less crowding than Ta Prohm, though it features similar encroaching vegetation.
