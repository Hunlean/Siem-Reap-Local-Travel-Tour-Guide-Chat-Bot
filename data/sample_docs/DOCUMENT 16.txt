Name (EN): APOPO Visitor Center
Name (KM): មជ្ឈមណ្ឌលអ្នកទស្សនា អាប៉ូប៉ូ
Category: Museum / NGO
Latitude: 13.3653
Longitude: 103.8741

Description:
An educational facility detailing the impact of landmines in Cambodia. It showcases the work of APOPO's 'HeroRATs'—African giant pouched rats trained to sniff out landmines safely and efficiently. Visitors can watch live demonstrations of the rats at work.
