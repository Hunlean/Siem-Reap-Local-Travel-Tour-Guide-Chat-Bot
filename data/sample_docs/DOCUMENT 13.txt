Name (EN): Tonle Sap Lake
Name (KM): បឹងទន្លេសាប
Category: Nature / Local Village
Latitude: 12.9833
Longitude: 103.8333

Description:
The largest freshwater lake in Southeast Asia and an ecological hotspot. Its size fluctuates dramatically with the seasons. It is famous for its stilted and floating villages, where entirely community lives on the water, relying on the lake's abundant fisheries.
