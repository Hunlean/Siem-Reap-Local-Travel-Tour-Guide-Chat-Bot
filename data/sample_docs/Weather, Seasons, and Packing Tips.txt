Title: Siem Reap Climate and Best Times to Visit

Topic: Weather, Seasons, and Packing Tips
Language Support: English & Khmer (ភាសាខ្មែរ)

Description:
Siem Reap has a tropical climate with two distinct seasons: wet and dry.
1. Cool Dry Season (November to February): This is the peak tourist season. Temperatures are pleasant (ranging from 22°C to 30°C) with low humidity and almost no rain. It is the most comfortable time for temple trekking, but popular spots will be crowded.
2. Hot Dry Season (March to May): Temperatures soar, often reaching up to 40°C. Humidity is high, making temple climbs physically demanding. Visiting early in the morning (5:00 AM to 9:00 AM) or late afternoon is highly recommended to avoid the midday heat.
3. Rainy/Wet Season (June to October): Marked by short, heavy afternoon downpours. The landscape becomes lush, green, and incredibly picturesque. Moats around Angkor Wat fill up, creating beautiful reflections, and there are far fewer tourists.
4. Packing Checklist: Light, breathable cotton clothing, sunblock, a wide-brimmed hat, a reusable water bottle, insect repellent (crucial for jungle temples), and a lightweight rain poncho if traveling during the monsoon.
