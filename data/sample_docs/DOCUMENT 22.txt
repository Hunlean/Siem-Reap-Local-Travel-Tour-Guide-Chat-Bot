Name (EN): Ta Som
Name (KM): ប្រាសាទតាសោម
Category: Historical Site
Latitude: 13.4645
Longitude: 103.9051

Description:
A small, quiet temple located at the eastern end of the Northern Baray. It is most famous for the massive, ancient strangler fig tree completely overwhelming its eastern gopura (gateway), making for an incredibly dramatic and photogenic scene without the heavy crowds.
