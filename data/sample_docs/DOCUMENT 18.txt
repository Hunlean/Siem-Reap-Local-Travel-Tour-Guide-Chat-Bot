Name (EN): Angkor National Museum
Name (KM): សារមន្ទីរជាតិអង្គរ
Category: Museum
Latitude: 13.3667
Longitude: 103.8601

Description:
An archaeological museum dedicated to the collection, preservation, and presentation of Angkorian artifacts. It provides excellent historical context before visiting the temples, featuring multimedia presentations, thousands of Buddha statues, and ancient steles.
