Name (EN): Banteay Kdei
Name (KM): ប្រាសាទបន្ទាយក្តី
Category: Historical Site
Latitude: 13.4299
Longitude: 103.8986

Description:
Known as the 'Citadel of Monks,' this sprawling, largely unrestored monastic complex provides a quiet, maze-like exploration experience. Built in the late 12th to early 13th centuries, it features beautiful entry towers displaying the face of Lokesvara.
