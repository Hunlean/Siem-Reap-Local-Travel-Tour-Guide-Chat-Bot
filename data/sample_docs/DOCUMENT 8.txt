Name (EN): West Baray
Name (KM): បារាយណ៍ខាងលិច
Category: Hidden Gem / Nature
Latitude: 13.4333
Longitude: 103.8

Description:
An immense rectangular artificial lake (baray) constructed in the 11th century. Measuring 8km by 2.2km, it is a testament to the advanced hydrology engineering of the Khmer Empire. Today, it is a quiet spot for locals to swim, relax on hammocks, and watch the sunset.
