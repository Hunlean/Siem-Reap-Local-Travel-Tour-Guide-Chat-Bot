Name (EN): Kulen Elephant Forest
Name (KM): ព្រៃដំរីគូលែន
Category: Nature / Sanctuary
Latitude: 13.5186

Description:
A sanctuary located in the foothills of Phnom Kulen offering a peaceful retirement for former riding elephants. Visitors can observe the elephants interacting naturally in the forest environment, feed them, and walk alongside them, promoting ethical wildlife tourism.
