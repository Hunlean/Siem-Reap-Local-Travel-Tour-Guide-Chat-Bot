Name (EN): Angkor Night Market
Name (KM): ផ្សាររាត្រីអង្គរ
Category: Shopping
Latitude: 13.3562
Longitude: 103.8531

Description:
The original night market in Siem Reap, featuring over 200 bamboo huts selling traditional handicrafts, silk paintings, clothing, and souvenirs. It also houses several massage parlors and small bars, providing a relaxed shopping environment after a day of temple exploration.
